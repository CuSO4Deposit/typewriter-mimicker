TYPEWRITER MIMICKER
===================

This is a small RFC-ish plain text sample. The formatter treats normal
paragraph text as soft-wrapped prose, but keeps indented text stable so
tables, code, and literal examples survive the trip to paper.

- keep the formatter deterministic
- keep the renderer responsible for ink and paper
- keep the input plain

    GET /typed-page HTTP/1.1
    Host: example.test

The visual character comes later. At this stage each character only needs
to know its page, row, column, style, and seed.
